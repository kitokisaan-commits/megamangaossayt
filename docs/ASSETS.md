# Development artwork

The three covers in `public/demo/` were generated specifically for this project as original compositions: an adult lantern courier, an adult sailor and two adult university students. They are not copied panels or covers from an existing manga franchise. No existing character names, logos or franchise styling were requested.

The development seeder uses those covers to create clearly labelled reader test pages. These pages exercise image dimensions, ordering and loading; they are not a claim that full original manga chapters were authored. Replace the development series with your own permitted content before a public launch.
